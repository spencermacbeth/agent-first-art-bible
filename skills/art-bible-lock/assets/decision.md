# Art-direction decision

## Identity and authority

Decision ID, date, reviewer and source feedback. Link the current bible section and reviewed candidate/artifact identities.

## Accepted scope

Name the accepted properties and stage: reference, concept, geometry, material, motion, sound or integrated presentation. Separate a recommendation from the owner's decision.

## Evidence and review conditions

Link the reviewed media or scene, artifact hashes, recipe, viewing conditions and relevant technical checks. State which checks remain unavailable when they limit acceptance.

## Required changes and open questions

Carry accepted properties into the next brief. Identify what must change, why, and how it will be inspected.

## Superseded direction

Link the prior instruction or artifact, why it changed, and the retained baseline.

## Active resource update

Record the authoritative bible change, accepted reference paths and any runtime integration that was actually reviewed. Keep pending stages explicit.

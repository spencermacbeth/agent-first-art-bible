# Reference catalog

Keep original files in stable locations. The columns below are a suggested record, not a required database schema.

| Reference ID | Original path | Origin and usage constraints | Property to carry forward | Property to avoid copying | Related task or decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For derived crops, excerpts or normalized audio, record the original ID, transform and output path. Record time ranges for video or sound references. Link a selected reference to the scope of its approval; presence in this catalog alone does not make it accepted direction.

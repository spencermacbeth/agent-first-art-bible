# Reference catalog

Keep original files in stable locations under `references/originals/`; put crops, excerpts and normalized audio under `references/derived/`. The columns below are a suggested record, not a required schema.

| Reference ID | Original path | Origin and usage constraints | Property to carry forward | Property to avoid copying | Related task or decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For a derived reference, record the source ID, the transform and the output path. Record time ranges for video or sound references.

Presence here is not acceptance. A reference becomes direction when an entry or a decision names it and states the scope.

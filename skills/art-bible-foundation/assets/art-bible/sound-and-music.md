# Sound and music

## Identity

For music: instrumentation, texture, tempo range and the emotional function of each context. For sound effects: what they are made of and what they never sound like. Name the reference that defines each.

## Entries

One entry per family: a music bed per context, a sound-effect family per system. Use the five headings from [visual-direction.md](visual-direction.md). Review conditions for sound include gain, normalization, the event or mix it plays in, loop and tail behavior, and the output device. A sound that works alone can wear out when it repeats during play; say where repetition is checked.

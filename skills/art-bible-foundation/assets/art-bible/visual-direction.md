# Visual direction

## Identity

The visual properties that carry the game across every asset family: composition, palette, proportions, materials, lighting, motion. Name the reference that defines each.

## Entries

One entry per asset family: a character, an environment module, a prop class, a card frame. Use these five headings for every entry so a new session knows what it may change and where to look at the result.

### Family name

**Identity and function.** What it is in the game and what the player must read from it.

**Accepted references.** Stable paths or catalog IDs. Who accepted, when, and at which scope: reference, concept, geometry, material, motion or integrated presentation.

**Constraints and exclusions.** Silhouette, palette, material, composition, movement. What must never appear.

**Production and rendering recipe.** Links to the scripts, provider records, scene or playback setup actually used. Only the settings that apply to this family.

**Review conditions and remaining questions.** Where the result is inspected (scene, camera, display size) and what is still open.

## Superseded

Date, what changed, why, and what replaced it. Keep the artifact reachable.

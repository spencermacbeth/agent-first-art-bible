# Establish the art bible

Read the project's standing instructions and existing art, audio and presentation guidance. Identify the authoritative resource before creating another document. Preserve the owner's artistic intent, existing structure and accepted work.

## Establish the working context

Determine the asset family, its gameplay or communication role, current accepted references, unresolved choices and actual review conditions. Existing code and renders can answer platform and stack questions. Ask only for missing information that changes the direction or authorized work; continue organizing the available evidence meanwhile.

An art bible should let a new session find:

- The project's identity and the properties that carry it into each asset family.
- Accepted references, their stable paths and the scope of their approval.
- Constraints such as silhouette, palette, material, composition, movement or sonic character.
- The production recipe and the place where results are inspected.
- Open decisions and clearly marked superseded directions.

Use [the bible template](assets/ART-BIBLE.md) when the project lacks a coherent entry point. Adapt its headings to the project; a formal schema is not required. Put detailed job records beside the assets rather than expanding the active bible indefinitely.

## Assemble the seed library

Include the owner's drawings, photographs, location video, objects and recordings when relevant. Preserve originals. Annotate what each reference contributes: proportions, silhouette, material, spatial arrangement, rhythm, movement or sound. State what should not be copied. A creative seed is distinct from a numeric random seed.

Use [the reference catalog](assets/reference-catalog.md) to track stable IDs, paths, origin and intended use. Preserve known attribution and usage constraints; do not infer ownership from a file being present. Unresolved rights can remain marked unresolved while local analysis continues within the task's permission.

Avoid flattening all references into one averaged style. Group them by the question they answer. Give each asset task the relevant subset.

## Prepare the next task

Use [the task brief](assets/asset-task.md). Identify what the next pass is trying to learn, what stays fixed, candidate identity, review conditions and the authorized spending boundary. Choose a stack based on the media, the existing renderer and how easily the owner can inspect results. Reuse a suitable existing review tool.

Merge a short pointer to the authoritative bible into the instruction mechanism used by the current harness. Preserve unrelated instructions. Confirm the next task can identify the relevant current section; automatic loading varies by tool.

Deliver the bible entry point, the annotated reference paths and a concrete next task. Keep proposed direction visibly separate from accepted direction. Do not generate a paid batch merely because the documents now exist.

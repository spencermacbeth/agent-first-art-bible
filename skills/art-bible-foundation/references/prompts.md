# Starting an art bible

Adapt the paths and scope to your project.

## Establish the resource

```text
Help me organize an art bible for {project}. Start with my ideas and references
in {location}, including drawings, photos, recordings and existing assets.
Keep our existing direction where it is settled, and identify what to explore
next. Make the bible easy for future agents to find through the project's
normal instructions. Start with {subject}; if I have asked for a first taste
pass, carry it through to candidates and a running review interface.
```

# Feedback for the next pass

Keep the feedback with the candidates the user actually reviewed. A note should remain attached to that version when new work arrives.

Capture what they want to keep, change, combine or explore further. Preserve their words and any drawings, annotations or recordings they supplied. Ratings can help compare a batch, but a score alone rarely explains what to do next.

Make the feedback available to the next agent session, through project-local files or an export. The user should know how their notes reach the agent.

When acting on the feedback, distinguish a choice from a request to try something. "Keep the silhouette; try a matte finish" chooses the silhouette and starts a material experiment. Put the new version back in the review and retain the earlier comparison.
